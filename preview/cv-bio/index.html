CV / Bio

Nick Kuminoff is a Professor in the Department of Economics at Arizona State University and a Research Associate at the National Bureau of Economic Research. His research focuses on environmental economics. Professor Kuminoff’s work has been funded by the National Science Foundation, the US Environmental Protection Agency and the National Institutes of Health, and published in journals such as the American Economic Review, Review of Economic Studies, International Economic Review, Journal of Economic Literature, Journal of Environmental Economics and Management, Journal of Econometrics, Review of Environmental Economics and Policy, Land Economics and Journal of Urban Economics.

He holds a Ph.D. in Economics from North Carolina State University (2006) and MS (2000) and BS (1999) degrees from the University of California-Davis. He was previously an Assistant Professor at Virginia Tech.
